# Camera Controls and Presets

## Orbit Camera (Default)
- **Mouse Drag**: Orbit / rotate around the scene target.
- **Mouse Wheel**: Zoom in and out.

## Camera Presets (Reference Photos)
- **Key 1**: Front view (matching Photo 1 / IMG-20261104-WA0014.jpg)
- **Key 2**: Rear-quarter view (matching Photo 2 / IMG-20261104-WA0015.jpg)
- **Key 3**: Side view (matching Photo 3 / IMG-20261104-WA0016.jpg)
- **Key 4**: Three-quarter view (matching Photo 4 / IMG-20261104-WA0017.jpg)

## Follow Camera
- **Key F**: Toggles third-person follow camera mounted behind the moving excavator.
